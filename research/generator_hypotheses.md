# Generator hypothesis ledger (Phase 2)

Format per hypothesis: statement, historical evidence, implementation,
parameters searched, prediction, test result, verdict. A hypothesis is
**retained** only if it beats an appropriate random null model on
out-of-sample (walk-forward) prediction of withheld solved puzzles --
fitting the training data is not sufficient (see Phase 3/4 anti-bullshit
rule).

Populated by the Phase 2-4 research workflow.
